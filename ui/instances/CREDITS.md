# Instance art

Original pixel art made for this project, under the repository's licence. The banners of both
regions (`<instance>.png`, and `<instance>-fade.png` with its lower left darkened for the name drawn
over it, 192×72), the roadmap (`roadmap-1`, `-3`, `-6`: the road with the first site reached, three
cleared and all six cleared, 256×144), the Gloaming Isles' sea chart (`isles-map-1`, `-4`, `-8`: the
same three moments of its eight sites, 256×144), the Sunken Crypt's route (`route-start`, `-mid`,
`-boss`, 256×64) and the room icons (`icon-*.png`, 16×16) are drawn by `scripts/instance-art/`; see
the README there to change or add one.
