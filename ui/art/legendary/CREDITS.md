# Legendary art

Original pixel art made for this project, under the repository's licence. Each item is drawn at
32×32 by `scripts/legendary-art/relics.py`, which also draws the two frames; see the README there
to change or add one. `<id>.png` is the item and `<id>-glow.png` the same with its glow.

The six relics of the instances (`tidewake-bell`, `heart-of-the-last-pour`,
`amber-of-the-sleeping-queen`, `rimebitten-horn`, `drowned-concordance`,
`fang-of-the-coiled-wyrm`) are drawn the same way by `scripts/instance-art/rewards.py`.
