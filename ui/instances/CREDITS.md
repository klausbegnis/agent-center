# Instance art

Original pixel art made for this project, under the repository's licence. The six banners
(`<instance>.png`, and `<instance>-fade.png` with its lower left darkened for the name drawn over
it, 192×72), the roadmap (`roadmap-1`, `-3`, `-6`: the road with the first site reached, three
cleared and all six cleared, 256×144), the Sunken Crypt's route (`route-start`, `-mid`, `-boss`,
256×64) and the room icons (`icon-*.png`, 16×16) are drawn by `scripts/instance-art/`; see the
README there to change or add one.
